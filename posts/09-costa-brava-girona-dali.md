# Costa Brava Below Girona: Roses, Cadaqués, Lloret, Tossa & the Dali Trail

[IMAGE]

The Costa Brava — the "wild coast" — is more than the package-holiday strip you've heard about. It's a string of very different worlds: the golden beaches, the surreal white village of Cadaqués, the fishing port of Roses, the party town of Lloret, the movie-scarred coves of Tossa, and hidden inland, Girona and the Dali museum. This is the whole spread, and you can do it in a measure.

## Roses — the wide bay & the fortress

[IMAGE]

Roses welcomes you with a generous, wide **bay** — the *Roses bay*, families, sailing, good sand. And it holds a real surprise: the **Citadel of Roses** (Ciutadella de Roses), the military citadel with a decent little museum of the town, all set the sea. It's a friendly, well-rounded spot, and the gulf itself is the beauty.

**Best for:** the bay, the sailing energy, the citadel, the seafood.

## Cadaqués — the white village on the sea

[IMAGE]

Cadaqués is the painter's village of Salvador Dalí — the surrealist's other-life. A white village climbing around a calm crescent bay, with the tiny old church and the timeless lanes. Dalí spent time here; it's the closest the coast has to a Mediterranean *stay charming* fine.

**Best for:** the quiet bay, the art atmosphere, the Cas d'en Salad / the fishermen's-mujanto of Dalí's house *just* outside at Portlligat (worth the short ride).

## Lloret de Mar — the vivid famous one

The hugely famous, big, package-beach resort. Not subtle — but if you want the full "Costa Brava resorts," a buzzing seaside, nightlife, a huge sandy beach — this is it. Unfiltered.But honest: it's the party end; great if that's your plan, offered if you want quiet.

## Tossa de Mar and the *Vell* (the walled cove)

[IMAGE]

**Tossa de Mar**, and its old town, is the movie in person — the *walled old town*, seasticled, wrapped in medieval walls with a *t**-resident cove beach. It's cinematic (yes, it's stood for several films, incl. a famous touch of *The Game of Thrones*-era and golden-age film references). Best fix: climb the walls to the lighthouse for the whole cove.

## Figueres & the Dalí Theatre-Museum — the surreal total

[IMAGE]

Just inland, **Figueres** is home to the **Dalí Theatre-Museum** — the museum *Dalí built* to be his surrealist masterpiece, the hemispherical glass dome, the giant eggs, the surrealist room, the complete works. You arrive not to a museum but to a dream-installation. Worth a whole afternoon.

## Girona — the "Game of Thrones" church & the walk over the river

[IMAGE]

And **Girona**, an hour up — the old city on the river, with the many-coloured **houses on the Onyar**, the cathedral with one of the widest single Gothic naves in the world, and — yes — the **cathedral steps** and many streets used in *Game of Thrones*. The trope "GoT church" is real here. Climb to the cathedral square, walk the walls for the town on the river below sunset.

## The practical bit

- **Ct hashCode:** you need a car or very good bus timing for the full spread; the four coast villages are manageable with the regional buses from Figueres/Girona, but a car turns a puzzle into a joy.
- **Day-planning:** Girona + Figueres is a fine *day* from the coast; Cadaqués + Roses is another; Toccan + Lloret.
- **Season:** shoulder (May, Sept) balances the crowds of Lloret and cools the inland museum day.

## What I'd do differently

I won't skip **Cadaqués** even though it's the detour — the Pati that the package resorts advertise is legend. And do Figueres + Girona as a *pair*, museum morning, city sunset — it's the trip's backbone.
